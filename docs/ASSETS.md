# Demo photography

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. These are fictional illustrative catalogue images, not photographs of the user's actual sarees. Each demo product and the demo storefront are labelled accordingly.

Files under `frontend/public/images/`:

- `maroon.webp`: folded maroon silk with antique-gold floral-inspired border.
- `emerald.webp`: emerald silk with antique-gold lotus-inspired border.
- `pink.webp`: blush silk with a contrasting maroon/gold border.
- `gold.webp`: ochre-gold silk with a plum floral-inspired border.

Prompt direction: “Use case: product-mockup. One premium photorealistic ecommerce photograph of a [colour and border described above] silk saree, folded and cascading on a warm ivory limestone pedestal. Fine natural silk texture, intricate Indian motifs, soft side daylight, neutral warm background. Portrait 3:4 product composition. No text, people, logos or collage. Fictional demonstration catalogue asset.”

The generated PNGs were resized and encoded as WebP for the explicitly requested image optimization; product designs were not edited. Upload real product views through the admin before selling. Separate body/border/pallu/blouse views are supported by the gallery and labelled uploads, but were not invented from a single fictional photo.
